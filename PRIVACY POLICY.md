# Privacy Policy

This document is about Libro de Esher's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect while operating our website. This Privacy Policy applies to github.com/softwaremagico/LibrodeesherNG-Android (hereinafter, "us", "we", or "github.com/softwaremagico/LibrodeesherNG-Android" or "the application"). We have adopted this privacy policy ("Privacy Policy") to explain what information may be collected by this application. This Privacy Policy applies only to information we collect through the use of this application and does not apply to our collection of information from other sources like githubs comments sections or any other external source not directly connected with the use of this application.

## What we collect

The Software "Libro de Esher" is used for creating fictional Rolemaster characters and therefore does not store, collects or share any personal data. Characters, options and random preferences are kept locally on your device (private app database, private preferences and temporary cache files used only to export or share JSON, TXT and PDF files when you explicitly choose those actions). "Libro de Esher" neither uses any kind of third-party tools for advertisments, marketing like Adwords or any other that can collect any personal data from your device. This software neither requires any kind of registration or payment. The application does not request Internet, location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions, does not send data to any server controlled by us and does not include analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. Diagnostic logs are written only to the device log and are not transmitted by us. Cloud backup is explicitly disabled for the application's database, preferences and files, both through `data_extraction_rules.xml` (Android 12 and later) and `backup_rules.xml` (older devices): characters, options and random preferences are never uploaded to a cloud backup. On Android 12 and later, those same local records may move to a new device only through Android's encrypted device-to-device transfer. Exported `.rlm`, JSON, TXT and PDF files leave the app only when you explicitly use the share or export action.
